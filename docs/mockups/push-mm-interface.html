Stylized to scale — not a literal render of Push 3's industrial design.

Knobs

  • 8 encoders drive the current page's params, always for whichever track is selected — turn one, it sends that param's real Monomachine MIDI CC for that track.
  • On SEQ, encoder 1 is repurposed as BASE CHANNEL (0–15) — no spare SETTINGS column was left for it.

Pages

  • 2 banks × 8 top-screen tabs. SYNTH · AMP · FILTER · EFFECTS · LFO1 · LFO2 · LFO3 · LEVEL cover the 58 real per-track params.
  • SEQ and SETTINGS are pinned in both banks, same slot, so they're always one press away.

Menus

  • SEQ's bottom row: PAGE (steps 1–8 ↔ 9–16), PLAY, STOP, REC.
  • SETTINGS: 4 columns — MIDI IN, AUDIO OUTPUT, AUDIO CHANNEL, MIDI CHANNEL — each its own encoder + commit button.

Pads — dual mode

  • Any other page: pads play the selected track live. Unlit — no LED meaning is assigned outside SEQ.
  • SEQ page: rows 0–5 (bottom-up) = tracks 1–6's step grid, row 7 = per-track mute, row 6 reserved. A tap simulates the real Track-select + Trigger-key panel press — mdLib's own sequencer, not a reimplementation.
